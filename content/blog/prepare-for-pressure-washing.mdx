## Preparing for Your Pressure Washing Service

To ensure the best results from your pressure washing appointment, there are a few simple steps you can take to prepare.

### Before We Arrive

- **Move vehicles** away from driveways and parking areas being cleaned
- **Close windows and doors** to prevent water from entering your home
- **Remove personal items** from patios, decks, and walkways
- **Secure pets** inside during the cleaning process
- **Note any areas of concern** to discuss with our team

### During the Service

Our team will handle everything from setup to cleanup. We use regulated PSI settings appropriate for each surface type to ensure thorough cleaning without damage.

### After the Cleaning

Allow cleaned surfaces to dry completely before walking or driving on them. You'll notice an immediate improvement in the appearance of your property.
