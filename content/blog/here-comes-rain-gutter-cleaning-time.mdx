## Time to Get Your Gutters Ready for the Rain

As fall arrives in the Lower Mainland and Fraser Valley, leaves begin to drop and your gutters start collecting debris. This is the most important time of year to have your gutters professionally cleaned.

Clogged gutters can lead to water overflow, which may damage your home's foundation, fascia boards, and landscaping. Pure Pressure recommends scheduling your gutter cleaning in the fall after most leaves have dropped but before the heavy rains begin.

### Why Fall Gutter Cleaning Matters

- Prevents water damage to your home's exterior
- Protects your foundation from water pooling
- Extends the life of your gutter system
- Prevents ice dams in colder weather
