## Protect Your Rental Property Investment

As a landlord or property manager, maintaining the exterior of your rental property is crucial for both tenant satisfaction and property value. Regular exterior cleaning helps prevent costly repairs down the road.

### Why Exterior Maintenance Matters for Rentals

- Increases curb appeal and property value
- Prevents long-term damage from moss, algae, and debris
- Keeps tenants happy and reduces turnover
- Addresses maintenance before it becomes a major issue
