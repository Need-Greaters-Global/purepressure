## Moss on Your Roof? Time to Act

If your roof looks like it's growing a green carpet, you're not alone. The wet climate in the Lower Mainland and Fraser Valley creates ideal conditions for moss growth on roofs.

### Why Moss is a Problem

Moss holds moisture against your roof surface, which can lead to:

- Shingle deterioration and lifting
- Water infiltration under roofing materials
- Reduced roof lifespan
- Unsightly appearance that affects curb appeal
