Every year we receive many requests for pressure washing, brush washing siding, and window cleaning services. It is our sincere desire to provide quality service to every one of our customers in a timely fashion. We recommend that you schedule your spring cleaning service now to ensure that you get an appointment that works best with your schedule.

March, April, May, and June are the best months to have your pressure washing, brush washing siding and window cleaning done. If you wait too long we will have scheduled appointments during this time frame and you run the risk of not getting an appointment until the summer months.

In recent years our summers have been very dry and in 2015 we even had a drought, resulting in a water ban that prevented us from working using water. These hot dry months are perfect for doing Moss Removal Roof Cleaning and this puts, even more, pressure on the schedule. We want to thank all of our customers for your business and we hope that these suggestions will help you plan your exterior building cleaning for 2017.
