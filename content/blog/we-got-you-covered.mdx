## Gentle Cleaning for Your Home's Exterior

Not all exterior surfaces can handle high-pressure washing. Vinyl siding, painted surfaces, and older materials require a gentler approach — and that's exactly what our brush washing service provides.

### What is Brush Washing?

Brush washing uses low-pressure water-fed poles with soft bristle brushes and gentle soap to clean your home's siding without risking damage. It's the safest method for:

- Vinyl siding
- Painted wood surfaces
- Stucco
- Delicate trim and decorative elements

### The Process

1. Apply gentle soap solution
2. Scrub with soft bristle brushes on water-fed poles
3. Rinse with low-pressure water
4. Final inspection for spotless results
