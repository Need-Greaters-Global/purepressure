**Pure Pressure has a BIG ANNOUNCEMENT!**

As of January 1st, 2016 Pure Pressure is proud to announce that they have acquired and merged with Irvine Sales and Service BC. Jamie and Jennifer Irvine were trained by James MacIntyre years ago and are so excited to join the Pure Pressure management team.

This merger makes Pure Pressure one of the leading exterior building cleaning companies in the Lower Mainland and Fraser Valley. What does this mean for you?

1. A larger customer service department focused on taking care of your needs.
2. A larger service area, Pure Pressure now services the Sea-to-Sky area, Lower Mainland and Fraser Valley.
3. More trucks on the road, Pure Pressure now has 6 teams of professionally trained exterior cleaning specialists.
4. Introduction of new services including; moss removal roof cleaning and installation of a 10-year NO CLOG Gutter System.

2016 is going to be a banner year for the Pure Pressure family and we look forward to the opportunity to work for you this year.
