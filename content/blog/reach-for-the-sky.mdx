## Multi-Story Pressure Washing

Some buildings are harder to reach than others. Pure Pressure has invested in the right equipment and training to safely clean multi-story buildings up to 5 levels high.

### Our High-Reach Capabilities

- Extension poles for reaching upper levels
- Boom lifts for safe access to high areas
- Regulated PSI to protect building surfaces
- WorkSafe BC certified for elevated work

### Safety First

Working at height requires proper training and equipment. Pure Pressure's team is fully certified and insured, with comprehensive fall protection training and WorkSafe BC compliance.

Whether your building is a single-story home or a multi-level strata complex, Pure Pressure has the tools and experience to get the job done safely and effectively.
